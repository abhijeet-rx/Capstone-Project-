Why it matters

Sensors drift long before failure. Ordinary alarms wait for breakdown.

Railway air systems
The compressor driven Air Production Unit powers brakes, suspension, and doors. A failure can take a train out of service.

Oil warms. Pressure behavior shifts. Motor current changes. These small signals can build for hours before a fixed tripwire catches up.

Can a compact, explainable health index flag progressive deterioration early while keeping false warnings manageable?

The twist

Good validation scores were a trap.

Several benchmark models ranked well in validation, then dropped below the test prevalence baseline. The dashed marker shows chance level at 1× prevalence.

Average precision relative to test prevalence

Isolation Forest ranked at 4.75× prevalence but produced no qualifying persistent warning at its evaluated threshold. MLP had the highest validation AP (0.068948) and fell to 0.53× prevalence on test.

The idea · Health Index

Five sensor signals, one readable alarm score.

Each rolling feature is scaled against its healthy 95th percentile, capped, then averaged. Three consecutive samples above Q99 establish an alarm episode.

What this does not show

Read the result with its boundaries in view.

Event 4 is one held-out failure. It does not establish performance across every compressor or failure mode.

No measured reduction in downtime or maintenance cost is demonstrated here.

SHAP describes model attribution; it does not establish physical causes of failure.

This is a health index, not a Remaining Useful Life estimate.

Findings apply to this dataset, features, thresholds, and evaluation workflow.
